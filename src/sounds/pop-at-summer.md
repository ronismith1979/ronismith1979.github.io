Originated from Bandung, Indonesia. Breathing the air of summer, crying the rain of fall. Giving you the breeze of summer, Pop at Summer spit rhymes out of their heart. Carrying the sweet persona of twee pop and indie pop. The band consist of the songwriter Givani ‘Avi’ Maghfirah Putri, Roni ‘Smi7h’ Tresnawan and M Rifky ‘KIIVV’ Adam Rahman. Their catchy lyrics will put your heart in a roller-coaster.

With the touch of sweet reverb-ish guitars, strong yet sweet vocals, and orchestral arrangements, their sound scape will eventually reach deep within your mind.
